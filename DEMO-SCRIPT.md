## Research behind the direction

The official 2025 PayPal Dev Days results name CrawlerPal, Codebusters and Rosen. These were winners of a different event, not this 2026 hackathon. They show several viable styles: a focused AI/payments workflow, a concrete integration upgrade, and a payment-native application. The transferable lesson is to demonstrate a useful workflow and make PayPal's role visible, rather than copy their concepts.

This year's project gallery was not public when researched. No claim is made that Second Socket is globally unprecedented or guaranteed to win. Its distinguishing feature is whole-bench donor allocation with evidence before checkout, grounded in Liam's computer-building experience.
